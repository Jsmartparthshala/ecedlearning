Add a device

Pick the school, then type the 8-character code the television or phone is showing on its activation screen. The device is saved against that school and wakes up by itself within a couple of seconds. Nobody at the school types anything, and no code is ever issued from here — it always comes off the device.

Leave the expiry blank for a permanent installation. Set it for a loaner or a term's trial and the television returns to its pairing screen on its own that evening, without anybody having to remember to come back here.

Assigning a teacher is optional and can be changed later from the table below. A television with no teacher belongs to the school and shows the school name, which is right for a hall or a shared room.

Type a title and press Enter, or click away — each one saves on its own. The list is the lessons that have a video, because those are the ones a child actually sees a name for; tick the box above to reach the rest.

The Nepali column can be left empty. A television shows the Nepali title when there is one and the English title when there is not, so a half-filled column is a usable state, not a broken one.

Where the televisions are

    A dot is a place, not a school — several schools in one district share one. Most are placed from the municipality written on the school, which puts them in the right district but not on the right street. A school with its own coordinates is placed exactly.

    A dot pulses while a lesson is playing on one of its televisions. A still dot means no lesson is playing there right now; it does not mean the television is switched off, because a television sitting on the browse screen reports nothing at all.
